# How Near Is GOD? Between the Human Search for Intermediaries and the Near Lord

**Author:** Berk KÖKSAL  
**Date:** 10.09.2026  
**Place:** Mersin

The main purpose of this article is to reflect on the verses together with other related verses and to explore, in a sincere and profound language, what witnessing the oneness of GOD means in human life in terms of knowledge, justice, truthfulness, and submission.

Peace. Peace be upon you.

Human beings seek intermediaries when they assume something is distant. Religious life can reproduce the same habit: figures, names, and authorities may be imagined as necessary bridges to GOD.

Yet 2:186 is direct: when the servants ask about GOD, He says that He is always near and answers the prayer of the caller. 50:16 says He knows the human being's innermost whisper and is nearer than the jugular vein. 56:85 speaks of nearness even at the moment of death.

These verses do not provide a physical theory of divine location, and this article will not invent one. Their relational implications are already profound: GOD knows, hears, answers, and does not authorize the human claim that idols are needed to bring us closer to Him. In fact, 39:3 explicitly criticizes that claim.

## 1. “I Am Always Near”

### Verse

> **2:186**
>
> وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِى عَنِّى فَإِنِّى قَرِيبٌ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ فَلْيَسْتَجِيبُوا۟ لِى وَلْيُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِى لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُونَ
>
> When  My  servants  ask  you  about Me, I am always near. I answer their prayers  when they pray  to Me. The people shall respond to Me and believe in Me, in order to be guided.
>
> Benim kullarım sana Beni sorduklarında, Ben her zaman yakınım. Bana dua ettiklerinde Ben onların dualarını yanıtlarım. İnsanlar da rehberlik edilmek için Bana yanıt versinler ve Bana inansınlar.

Source: <a href="https://kuranteyit.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">kuranteyit.com</a>

### Reflection on the Verse

2:186 answers the question about GOD with striking directness: “I am always near.” The verse then says that GOD answers the prayers of those who pray to Him.

The verse does not explain this nearness as physical distance, so it would be unwise to convert it into a spatial theory. But its relational meaning is clear: the caller does not speak into an unheard void.

The verse also asks the human being to respond to GOD and believe in Him. Nearness is not only comfort; it is a call that asks for a response.

**Verse references:** 2:186.

## 2. Nearness to the Innermost Whisper

### Verse

> **50:16** — We  created  the  human,  and  we know what he whispers to himself. We are closer to him than his jugular vein.

### Reflection on the Verse

50:16 says that GOD created the human being, knows what the self whispers, and is nearer than the jugular vein.

The verse joins nearness with knowledge. What is not spoken to another person is not hidden from GOD.

This brings both comfort and accountability. Divine nearness is more than a feeling of presence; it means the most inward region of the self is not outside divine knowledge.

**Verse references:** 50:16.

## 3. Near Even at Death

### Verse

> **56:85** — We are closer to it than you are, but you do not see.

### Reflection on the Verse

56:85 speaks in the context of death and says, “We are closer to him than you are, but you do not see.”

Human companions may stand physically beside the dying person, yet the verse points to a nearness they cannot see.

Its mode is not explained, so speculation should stop there. The clear point is that death does not place the person outside GOD's sovereignty or knowledge.

**Verse references:** 56:85.

## 4. The Lord Who Is Near and Responsive

### Verse

> **11:61** — To  Thamoud  we  sent  their  brother Sãleh. He said,  “O  my  people, worship  GOD;  you have no other god beside  Him.  He  initiated  you  from the earth, then settled you in it. You shall  seek  His  forgiveness,  then repent  to  Him.  My  Lord  is  always near, responsive.”

### Reflection on the Verse

11:61 brings monotheism, creation, repentance, and nearness into one call: “My Lord is always near, responsive.”

Nearness is therefore not detached from moral return. The verse joins it to asking forgiveness and repenting.

A near Lord is not merely a comforting idea. He is the Lord to whom the person returns, responds, and becomes accountable.

**Verse references:** 11:61.

## 5. Hearer, Near

### Verse

> **34:50** — Say, “If I go astray, I go astray because of my own shortcomings. And if I am guided, it is because of my Lord’s inspiration.  He is Hearer, Near.”

### Reflection on the Verse

34:50 attributes guidance to the Lord's inspiration and describes Him as Hearer, Near.

Nearness and hearing appear together. The effectiveness of prayer is not measured by how many people hear my voice or how impressive my words sound publicly.

Being heard by people and being heard by GOD are not the same thing.

**Verse references:** 34:50.

## 6. An Act That Brings Nearness

### Verse

> **9:99** — Other Arabs do believe in GOD and the Last Day, and consider their spending  to  be  a  means  towards GOD, and a means of supporting the messenger. Indeed, it will bring them nearer; GOD will admit them into His mercy. GOD is Forgiver, Most Merciful.

### Reflection on the Verse

9:99 describes people who regard their spending as a means toward GOD and states that it brings them nearer.

Nearness is therefore not only an emotion or statement. It is connected with righteous action and sacrifice.

This prevents the idea of divine nearness from becoming passive. 2:186 likewise asks people to respond to GOD.

**Verse references:** 9:99.

## 7. “Implore Me, and I Will Respond to You”

### Verse

> **40:60** — Your Lord says, “Implore Me, and I will respond to you. Surely, those who are too arrogant to worship Me will enter Gehenna, forcibly.”

### Reflection on the Verse

40:60 commands people to call upon GOD and promises a response, then warns against the arrogance of refusing worship.

The human search for intermediaries may sometimes arise from feeling too distant or unworthy to turn directly to GOD. The verse points the call directly to Him.

This directness does not exalt the human being. It humbles him, because the answer comes from GOD's mercy, not human power.

**Verse references:** 40:60.

## 8. Turning to GOD Publicly and Privately

### Verse

> **7:55** — You shall worship your Lord publicly and privately; He does not love the transgressors.

### Reflection on the Verse

7:55 commands worship of the Lord publicly and privately and rejects transgression.

Religious life is not real only when witnessed by a community. The relationship continues in solitude.

Divine nearness therefore places the private self under the same reality as the public self: even when no human authority sees, GOD is not absent.

**Verse references:** 7:55.

## 9. Reverence and Hope

### Verse

> **7:56** — Do not corrupt the earth after it has been set straight, and worship Him out of reverence, and out of hope. Surely, GOD’s mercy is attainable by the righteous.

### Reflection on the Verse

7:56 commands worship with reverence and hope and says GOD's mercy is accessible to the righteous.

Nearness is not casual familiarity without awe. Reverence remains. Yet it is not terror without hope either.

The two together protect the heart from both trivializing GOD and despairing of Him.

**Verse references:** 7:56.

## 10. “We Idolize Them Only to Bring Us Closer to GOD”

### Verse

> **39:3** — Absolutely,  the  religion  shall  be devoted to GOD alone. Those who set up idols beside Him say, “We idolize them only to bring us closer to GOD; for they are in a better position!” GOD will judge them regarding their disputes. GOD does not guide such liars, disbelievers.

### Reflection on the Verse

39:3 states that religion must be devoted absolutely to GOD and reports the justification of idolizers: they claim the idols exist only to bring them closer to GOD.

This is crucial. The language of “closeness” itself does not guarantee a true religious path. A person can claim to seek GOD while constructing unauthorized intermediaries.

Read beside 2:186, the question becomes sharp: if GOD declares Himself near and responsive, why invent a bridge He did not authorize?

**Verse references:** 39:3.

## 11. All Intercession Belongs to GOD

### Verse

> **39:44** — Say,  “All  intercession  belongs  to GOD.”  To  Him  belongs  all  sovereignty  of  the  heavens  and the earth,  then  to  Him  you  will  be returned.

### Reflection on the Verse

39:44 says that all intercession belongs to GOD and all sovereignty of the heavens and the earth belongs to Him.

The verse returns ultimate authority to GOD. No being possesses an independent spiritual jurisdiction outside His will.

Nearness must therefore be understood inside monotheism, not inside a system that distributes divine authority among intermediaries.

**Verse references:** 39:44.

## 12. Call Upon No One Beside GOD

### Verse

> **72:18** — The  places  of  worship  belong  to GOD; do not call on anyone else beside GOD.

### Reflection on the Verse

72:18 states that the places of worship belong to GOD and commands people not to call upon anyone beside GOD.

The direction of worship and calling is explicit. Human beings may be honored, loved, or respected without becoming objects of religious invocation.

The point is not contempt for people but protection of the line of monotheism between the servant and GOD.

**Verse references:** 72:18.

## 13. “You Alone We Ask for Help”

### Verse

> **1:5** — You alone we worship. You alone we ask for help.

### Reflection on the Verse

1:5 directs worship and the request for help to GOD alone.

Ordinary human cooperation is not identical with worship; the verse speaks in the language of servitude and ultimate religious direction.

The question for me is therefore where final trust, rescue, and worship are placed in the heart.

**Verse references:** 1:5.

## 14. The Messenger Is Human; GOD Is One

### Verse

> **18:110** — Say,  “I  am  no  more  than  a  human like  you,  being  inspired  that  your god is  one god.  Those who hope to meet  their  Lord  shall  work  righteousness,  and  never  worship  any other god beside his Lord.”

### Reflection on the Verse

18:110 states that the messenger is a human being who receives inspiration that the god is one god, and commands those who hope to meet their Lord to live righteously without worshiping another beside Him.

Even the messenger is not transformed into a second divine center.

The message does not create a sacred intermediary to be worshiped; it directs the person toward the One GOD.

**Verse references:** 18:110.

## 15. Even Intercession Is Subject to His Will

### Verse

> **2:255** — GOD: there is no other god besides Him, the Living, the Eternal. Never a moment of unawareness or slumber overtakes Him. To Him belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth. Who could intercede with  Him,  except  in  accordance with His will? He knows their past, and their future. No one attains any knowledge, except as He wills. His dominion encompasses the heavens and the earth, and ruling them never burdens Him. He is the Most High, the Great.

### Reflection on the Verse

2:255 declares GOD living and eternal, owner of all things, and says no one can intercede except in accordance with His will.

This removes the idea of autonomous intercession. No being owns a spiritual power zone independent of GOD.

Divine nearness belongs within the same order of absolute sovereignty.

**Verse references:** 2:255.

## 16. Wherever You Are, He Is with You

### Verse

> **57:4** — He is the One who created the heavens and the earth in six days,* then assumed all authority. He knows everything that enters into the earth, and everything that comes out of it, and everything that comes down from the sky, and everything that climbs into it. He is with you wherever you may be. GOD is Seer of everything you do.

### Reflection on the Verse

57:4 describes GOD's knowledge over creation and says that wherever people are, He is with them, while also saying that He sees everything they do.

The verse does not explain the metaphysical mode of this “withness,” and it should not be turned into a physical theory.

What it clearly denies is the possibility of a place outside GOD's knowledge and sight.

**Verse references:** 57:4.

## 17. Secret Conversations Are Not Outside His Knowledge

### Verse

> **58:7** — Do  you  not  realize  that  GOD  knows everything  in  the  heavens  and  everything  on  earth?  No  three  people  can conspire  secretly  without  Him  being their  fourth,  nor  five  without  Him being  the  sixth,  nor  less  than  that, nor  more,  without  Him  being  there with  them  wherever  they  may  be. Then,  on  the  Day  of  Resurrection, He  will  inform  them  of  everything they  had  done.  GOD  is  fully  aware of all things.

### Reflection on the Verse

58:7 states that no secret conversation occurs, regardless of the number of people, without GOD being with them, and that He will inform them of their deeds on the Day of Resurrection.

Privacy from human beings is not privacy from GOD.

This makes divine nearness morally serious: the hidden self and the public self remain within the same divine knowledge.

**Verse references:** 58:7.

## 18. The Human Being Must Also Respond to Nearness

### Verse

> **2:186** — When  My  servants  ask  you  about Me, I am always near. I answer their prayers  when they pray  to Me. The people shall respond to Me and believe in Me, in order to be guided.

### Reflection on the Verse

2:186 does not end with GOD's nearness and response. It calls people to respond to Him and believe in Him so that they may be guided.

This keeps nearness from becoming spiritual consumption. The person is not merely reassured; he is addressed.

Prayer includes asking, but relationship with GOD also includes answering revelation with faith and action.

**Verse references:** 2:186.

## 19. Arabic Root Reflections: Nearness, Calling, Response, Oneness

### Verse

> **2:186** — When  My  servants  ask  you  about Me, I am always near. I answer their prayers  when they pray  to Me. The people shall respond to Me and believe in Me, in order to be guided.
>
> **50:16** — We  created  the  human,  and  we know what he whispers to himself. We are closer to him than his jugular vein.
>
> **39:3** — Absolutely,  the  religion  shall  be devoted to GOD alone. Those who set up idols beside Him say, “We idolize them only to bring us closer to GOD; for they are in a better position!” GOD will judge them regarding their disputes. GOD does not guide such liars, disbelievers.

### Reflection on the Verse

The Quranic field of nearness is connected with **ق ر ب (q-r-b)**. In 2:186 GOD declares Himself near; in 39:3 idolizers claim their idols will bring them nearer to GOD. The contrast is significant: revealed nearness versus a humanly manufactured theory of mediation.

Calling or prayer is connected with **د ع و (d-ʿ-w)**. In 2:186 the caller directs the call to GOD.

The language of response is connected with **ج و ب (j-w-b)**. The verse speaks both of GOD answering the caller and of people responding to GOD, creating a language of reciprocal address without equality of status.

The theological center remains oneness. Nearness does not multiply divine centers; it intensifies direct devotion to the One GOD.

**Verse references:** 2:186; 50:16; 39:3.

## Other Claims

Theological, mystical, and philosophical traditions have developed many different metaphysical accounts of divine nearness, “withness,” the phrase “nearer than the jugular vein,” and the nature of prayer. Some are symbolic, some ontological, and some devotional.

None of those systems determines the main reflection here. The direct verses are enough: 2:186 says GOD is near and answers prayer; 50:16 joins nearness with knowledge of the inner whisper; 56:85 speaks of nearness at death; and 39:3 criticizes the claim that idols are needed to bring people closer to GOD.

These verses do not justify a detailed theory of GOD's physical location or spatial mode. Their relational, epistemic, devotional, and monotheistic implications are already sufficient.

## Conclusion

How near is GOD? The Quran does not answer with a ruler. It does not give a spatial measurement. It says something more relevant to life: GOD is near, hears, knows, answers the caller, knows the innermost whisper, and remains sovereign even at the threshold of death.

This nearness calls me away from invented intermediaries and toward direct devotion. 39:3 criticizes idols justified as means of drawing nearer to GOD. 72:18 commands calling upon no one beside Him. 1:5 directs worship and ultimate help to GOD alone.

But nearness is not only a privilege; it demands a response. 2:186 calls people to respond to GOD and believe.

**Perhaps the decisive question is not only how near GOD is to me, but how sincerely I respond to the Lord who says He is near.**
