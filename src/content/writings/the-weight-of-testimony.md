**A Reflection on Verse 3:18**

**Location:** Mersin

### Verse

> **1:1** — In the name of GOD, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.*

## Introduction

**Peace. Peace be upon you.**

There are some verses that a person cannot simply read once and move past. Their words continue to circulate in the mind. With every return, they open another door and leave another question before us. For me, 3:18 is such a verse.

This verse does not merely declare the oneness of GOD. It speaks of testimony, knowledge, justice, power, and wisdom. Moreover, it connects all of them within a single sentence. The oneness of GOD is not left as a statement of belief; it is affirmed through testimony, upheld through justice, recognized through knowledge, and completed within power and wisdom.

At the beginning of the verse, GOD bears witness to His own oneness. Then the angels and those who possess knowledge join this testimony. It then declares that He is the absolute god, truthfully and equitably. At the end of the verse, the same truth is proclaimed once again: there is no god except Him.

It is clear that this repetition is not without purpose. The human mind is inclined to create partners, establish intermediaries, and elevate people and institutions beyond their proper place. For this reason, monotheism is not an idea to be stated once and then left behind. It is an awareness that must be remembered every day and rebuilt within the human being.

### Reflection on the Verse

GOD bearing witness to His own oneness shows that this truth does not depend on human acceptance. Even if all people were to say something else, there is no god except Him. Societies may exalt other beings, attribute sanctity to certain people, or make their traditions unquestionable. But none of these carries any weight before GOD’s testimony.

Here a person should ask himself a sincere question: Have the things I accept about GOD truly been shaped by the truth GOD has revealed, or by assumptions I inherited from my surroundings?

Verse 3:18 draws a person out of the safe space of habit. It asks us not to be satisfied with repeating someone else’s belief, but to establish our own testimony consciously. Bearing witness to the oneness of GOD is not merely saying a correct sentence; it is placing Him alone at the center of life.

## 2. The Truth Stated Twice

### Verses

> **3:2** — GOD: there is no god except Him; the Living, the Eternal.

> **3:6** — He is the One who shapes you in the wombs as He wills. There is no god except Him; the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **3:62** — Surely, this is the narration of the truth. Surely, there is no god except GOD. Surely, GOD is the Almighty, Most Wise.

### Reflection on the Verses

In 3:18, the statement “there is no god except Him” appears twice. In the first instance, testimony is brought forward; in the second, GOD’s power and wisdom are emphasized.

It is as though the verse closes every possible escape route that might open in the human mind. GOD’s oneness is first proclaimed, then the testimony of the angels and those who possess knowledge is mentioned. Justice is then recalled, and the same truth is stated again.

For this reason, the repetition in the verse is not merely a rhetorical ornament. It is a warning. A person must preserve monotheism not only on the tongue, but also in decisions.

## 3. The Greatest Testimony

### Verses

> **6:19** — Say, “Whose testimony is the greatest?” Say, “GOD’s. He is the witness between me and you that this Quran has been inspired to me so that I may preach it to you and to whomever it reaches. Indeed, you testify that there are other gods beside GOD.” Say, “I do not testify as you do; there is only one god, and I disown your idolatry.”

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

### Reflection on the Verses

This question is one that determines a person’s measure. There are many voices in life. Family speaks, society speaks, religious leaders speak, tradition speaks, and a person’s own fears and desires speak. Among all these voices, whose testimony will a person accept as the greatest?

There is a subtle but decisive distinction here. People also bear witness, but not every testimony is true. Verse 6:19 states that some people testify that there are other gods beside GOD. This means that presenting a statement as “testimony” does not make it true.

A person must choose the true testimony. This choice concerns not only the tongue, but the whole of life.

## 4. The Testimony and Obedience of the Angels

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **4:166** — But GOD bears witness concerning what He has revealed to you; He revealed it with His knowledge. And the angels also bear witness, but GOD is sufficient as a witness.

> **16:49** — Everything in the heavens and everything on earth—every creature—prostrates before GOD, and so do the angels, without the slightest arrogance.*

> **16:50** — They deeply revere their Lord, who is high above them, and they do what they are commanded to do.

### Reflection on the Verses

In 3:18, the angels bear witness to the oneness of GOD. In 4:166, they are said to bear witness to the revelation. Their testimony is not limited to words. Verses 16:49 and 16:50 show how this testimony turns into conduct: they prostrate, they are not arrogant, they deeply revere their Lord, and they do what they are commanded to do.

When a person says that there is no god except GOD, the natural consequence of that statement is submission. If one raises personal desire, social pressure, or the rulings of other people above what GOD has revealed, then the words spoken have not found their counterpart in life.

Bearing witness to the oneness of GOD is possible not by enlarging oneself, but by knowing one’s place. True submission does not diminish a person; it frees him from the burden of false greatness.

## 5. The Responsibility Carried by Knowledge

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **22:54** — Those who are blessed with knowledge will recognize the truth from their Lord; then they will believe in it, and their hearts will accept it without resistance. Surely, GOD guides the believers on the right path.

> **29:49** — In fact, these revelations are clear in the chests of those who possess knowledge. Only the wicked will disregard Our revelations.

### Reflection on the Verses

The fact that those who possess knowledge are mentioned together with the angels in 3:18 gives great value to knowledge. But at the same time, it turns knowledge into a serious responsibility.

Verse 29:49 states that the revelations are clear in the chests of those who possess knowledge. The expression “in the chests” is especially thought-provoking. It is not enough for knowledge to exist only on the tongue or in memory. It must settle into the inner world.

Sometimes a person explains to others what he knows but does not apply it to his own life. He speaks of justice but acts unjustly. He speaks of monotheism but exalts people. He speaks of submission but retreats when the matter touches his own interests.

## 6. Standing Firm in Knowledge

### Verses

> **3:7** — He sent down to you this scripture, containing verses that are easy to understand—which form the essence of the scripture—as well as verses that are multiple-meaning or allegorical. Those who harbor doubt in their hearts pursue the multiple-meaning verses in order to create confusion and extract a particular meaning. No one knows their correct meaning except GOD and those who are firmly grounded in knowledge. They say, “We believe in it—all of it comes from our Lord.” Only those who possess understanding will take heed.

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

### Reflection on the Verses

Being firmly grounded in knowledge does not mean claiming to know the answer to every question. In 3:7, the words of those firmly grounded in knowledge are extremely clear: “We believe in it—all of it comes from our Lord.”

Sometimes a person does not try to understand the revelation; instead, he tries to make the revelation say what he already thinks. He first makes his decision, then searches for pieces that will support it. In that case, the verses no longer guide the person; the person begins directing the verses according to his own desire.

A person firmly grounded in knowledge is someone who can say, “All of it comes from our Lord,” before saying, “I know.”

## 7. Justice Is Monotheism Made Visible in Life

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **6:152** — Do not touch the orphans’ money except in the most righteous manner until they reach maturity. When you trade, give full weight and full measure equitably. We never burden any soul beyond its means. When you bear witness, be absolutely just, even against your relatives. Fulfill your covenant with GOD. These are His commands to you, that you may take heed.

### Reflection on the Verses

In 3:18, GOD’s absolute divinity is not separate from the phrase “truthfully and equitably.” Monotheism and justice meet within the same sentence.

This connection is extremely important. A person may sometimes reduce faith to an intellectual acceptance. He says that GOD is one but cheats in trade. He prays but overlooks injustice when his own interests are involved. He uses religious expressions but favors his relatives when bearing witness.

Without justice, the statement of monotheism remains incomplete. The person who bears witness to the oneness of GOD must also uphold His justice in his own life.

## 8. Truthfulness and Inner Integrity

### Verses

> **3:17** — They are steadfast, truthful, submissive, charitable, and those who reflect at dawn.

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **9:119** — O you who believe, deeply revere GOD and be among the truthful.

### Reflection on the Verses

Immediately before 3:18, there is mention of people who are steadfast, truthful, submissive, charitable, and who reflect at dawn. This sequence contains an important indication of the character upon which testimony should rise.

But truthfulness is not merely the absence of lying. It is the existence of integrity between what a person says and how he lives. The fears, expectations, and loyalties of someone who says that there is no god except GOD should also confirm that statement.

Verse 9:119 commands being among the truthful. A person must carefully choose whom he keeps company with, whose words he listens to, and whom he takes as an example. Both truthfulness and crookedness can pass from one’s surroundings into the person.

## 9. Power Mentioned Together with Wisdom

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **3:126** — GOD thus informs you in order to give you good news and reassure your hearts. Victory comes only from GOD, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **2:32** — They said, “Glory be to You. We have no knowledge except what You have taught us. You are the All-Knowing, Most Wise.”

### Reflection on the Verses

Verse 3:18 ends with the words “the Almighty, Most Wise.” Power and wisdom are mentioned together.

In times of weakness, this truth gives reassurance. Sometimes circumstances appear to be against a person. Doors close, opportunities diminish, and a person sees the limits of his own strength. At that moment, remembering that victory comes only from GOD protects a person both from despair and from bowing before false powers.

No matter how much a person learns, he remains limited before the knowledge of GOD. Knowing this limit is not ignorance; it is wisdom.

## 10. Objecting Despite Knowledge

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **3:19** — The only religion approved by GOD is “Submission.” Ironically, those who received the scripture are the ones who objected to this truth out of jealousy, despite the knowledge they had received. As for those who reject GOD’s revelations, GOD is the strictest in reckoning.

### Reflection on the Verses

In 3:18, people of knowledge are counted among the witnesses to monotheism. The immediately following verse, 3:19, speaks of those who object to the truth despite having knowledge.

Here the difference appears between a person who truly possesses knowledge and someone who merely carries information. The first submits when he recognizes the truth. The second may become angry because the truth did not come from him, because it was communicated through someone else, or because it does not suit his interests.

When knowledge is not completed by submission, instead of bringing a person closer to GOD, it may imprison him within his own ego.

## 11. Not Taking People of Knowledge as Lords

### Verses

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **9:31** — They have taken their religious leaders and scholars as lords* instead of GOD. Others also deified the Messiah, son of Mary. All of them were commanded to worship one god. There is no god except Him. Glory be to Him; He is far above having any partner.

### Reflection on the Verses

Verse 3:18 gives value to people of knowledge. It counts them among those who bear witness to the oneness of GOD. But their position in the verse must be seen carefully: they are not GOD, they are not lords, and they are not an independent source of judgment beside GOD. They are witnesses.

Verse 9:31 clearly states the danger of taking religious leaders and scholars as lords instead of GOD.

True respect for a person of knowledge is not to deify him, but to take heed of his truthful testimony.

## 12. The Only Lord and Master

### Verses

> **2:107** — Are you not aware that GOD possesses the kingship of the heavens and the earth, and that you have no one besides GOD as your Lord and Master?

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **3:150** — Your Lord and Master is GOD alone, and He is the best supporter.

> **4:45** — GOD knows best who your enemies are. GOD is the only Lord and Master. GOD is the only Supporter.

### Reflection on the Verses

In 3:18, GOD is declared to be the “absolute god.” This absoluteness requires not placing another lord beside Him in any area of life.

Verse 2:107 says that there is no Lord and Master besides GOD. In 3:150, He is declared the best supporter, while 4:45 states that He is the only Lord, the only Master, and the only Supporter.

Human support can disappear in an instant. The opportunities we possess can be lost. People who appear powerful may come face to face with their own weakness. When a person sees how temporary all of these things are, he understands more clearly that true support comes only from GOD.

## 13. Living the Testimony

### Verses

> **3:17** — They are steadfast, truthful, submissive, charitable, and those who reflect at dawn.

> **3:18** — GOD bears witness that there is no god except He, and so do the angels and those who possess knowledge. Truthfully and equitably, He is the absolute god; there is no god but He, the Almighty, Most Wise.

> **5:119** — GOD will proclaim: “This is a day when the truthful will be saved by their truthfulness.” They have deserved gardens with flowing streams. They will remain there forever. GOD is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. This is the greatest victory.

### Reflection on the Verses

Testimony is not merely a statement spoken in a gathering or written in a text. A person’s entire life can become a testimony.

How we earn, how we spend, how we behave in a dispute, how we approach someone who is weak, what we do when we are alone, and where we stand when our interests are threatened all reveal our true testimony.

When a person bears witness to the oneness of GOD, he also places his own life under that statement. He can no longer easily defend lying, injustice, display, or taking people as lords. The words he speaks begin to question his behavior.

## Other Claims

Various interpretations have been put forward about 3:18 in different periods. In particular, different explanations have been offered regarding whom the expression “those who possess knowledge” includes, in what sense testimony is used, and the place of the expression of justice within the sentence.

Some approaches limit those who possess knowledge to a specific religious class. I believe this approach should be treated carefully. The verse itself presents people of knowledge not as an independent authority, but as those who bear witness to the oneness of GOD. Their value comes not from their titles, but from the harmony between their testimony and the truth.

Possessing knowledge does not give a person a separate authority beside GOD. On the contrary, it places that person under greater responsibility. The fact that 9:31 criticizes taking religious leaders and scholars as lords, while 3:18 counts people of knowledge among the witnesses, shows how important this distinction is.

Another approach is to read 3:18 merely as a theoretical statement declaring the principles of belief. Yet the direct presence of justice in the verse shows that monotheism cannot be considered separately from human conduct. Accepting the oneness of GOD changes a person’s understanding of justice, loyalties, and attitude toward authority.

The central thought emphasized in this article is this: 3:18 is not merely a verse that tells us who GOD is. It is also a mirror that measures a person’s knowledge, testimony, justice, and submission.

The different approaches mentioned in this section do not determine the main direction of the article. The main line is the clear connection the verses establish with one another and the reflection this connection awakens in the human being.

**Verse references: 3:18, 9:31.**

## Conclusion

Verse 3:18, although it appears short, carries a meaning that extends across the whole of a person’s life.

GOD bears witness to His own oneness. The angels and people of knowledge join this testimony. It is declared that He is the absolute god, truthfully and equitably. Then monotheism is proclaimed once again: there is no god except Him. He is the Almighty, Most Wise.

Each part of the verse completes the others.

- Testimony is incomplete without knowledge.

- Knowledge can turn into arrogance without submission.

- Monotheism finds no true counterpart in life without justice.

- Power gives reassurance when understood together with wisdom.

- Testimony loses its value without truthfulness.

Perhaps the most important question a person should ask himself before this verse is:

What am I truly bearing witness to?

Not only with my words, but with my choices, my fears, my loyalties, and my actions, whom do I treat as absolute?

Bearing witness to the oneness of GOD is not merely saying, “There is no god except Him.” It is refusing to give any other being the place that belongs to GOD. It means not taking people as lords, not turning knowledge into a tool of arrogance, not abandoning justice for the sake of self-interest, and standing behind what one knows to be true.

The testimony of the angels is confirmed through obedience, the testimony of people of knowledge through recognizing the truth, and the testimony of the human being through the life he lives.

For this reason, 3:18 is not merely a sentence to be read. It is a measure that must be lived again every day.

Throughout life, a person says many things. But some statements encompass a person’s entire life. “There is no god except Him” is one of them.

When this statement truly settles into the heart, a person’s fears change, loyalties change, understanding of justice changes, and the place given to other people changes. A person no longer sees any power as absolute. He does not regard any person as beyond question. He does not alter the truth for the sake of any interest.

Because he knows that the greatest testimony is GOD’s testimony.

And He bears witness that there is no god except Him.

**Verse references: 3:18, 6:19.**
